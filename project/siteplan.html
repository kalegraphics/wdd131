New Kingdom Church Abuja

Website Planning Document

Site Name

New Kingdom Church Abuja

I chose this name because it is my home church. This website will provide information about church activities, ministries, service schedules, events, and ways visitors can connect with the church community.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to help church members and visitors learn more about New Kingdom Church Abuja. The site will provide information about church services, ministries, upcoming events, and contact information.

Scenarios

  • What time does New Kingdom Church Abuja hold Sunday services?
  • What ministries are available for children, youth, and adults?
  • How can I contact the church or submit a prayer request?
